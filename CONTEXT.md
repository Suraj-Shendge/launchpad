# ProjectHub domain glossary

## User
A person with a ProjectHub account who can publish projects, participate in community features, and use platform services.

## Verification role
A ProjectHub identity designation that controls the verified tick shown with a user's public identity. It is independent of administrative access.

## GitHub verification
A verification state established when a user connects a GitHub identity to ProjectHub. It can make the user's earned Member, Founder, or Entrepreneur tier verified.

## Admin access
A separate authorization capability that allows a user to operate parts of the ProjectHub control room. Admin access does not imply a public verification role.

## Platform owner
The original ProjectHub administrator with unrestricted control over administrative access and platform operations.

## Admin role
A named administrative permission preset such as Admin, Moderator, Finance Admin, Content Admin, or Community Admin. A role is a convenience for defining capabilities; the user's actual permissions determine what they can do.

## Admin permission
A specific capability granted to an administrator, such as viewing users, moderating community content, managing auctions, or editing settings.

## Delegated administrator
A User who has Admin access granted by the Platform owner. Delegated administrators operate only within their granted permissions and optional expiration.

## Upvote
A single toggleable positive vote a User can give to a published Project. A User can have at most one upvote per Project. Upvotes are counted for the Project and can later support discovery/ranking.

## Follow User
A relationship where one User follows another User. It is a platform-wide relationship that can later power personalized project, community, and newsletter feeds.

## Follow Forum
A relationship where a User follows an active Community forum. Forum follows can generate meaningful in-app notifications for new discussions and important forum activity.

## Notification
A persisted in-app activity item delivered to a User. Notifications have a type, message, optional destination link, read state, and creation time. The notification system is designed to support future email/push delivery without changing the underlying event model.

## Newsletter
A ProjectHub-owned periodic digest curated and published by admins for email subscribers, with a public web archive for sent editions.

## Newsletter subscriber
A person identified by an email address who has opted into the ProjectHub Newsletter. A subscriber must confirm their email before receiving newsletters and can unsubscribe at any time.

## Newsletter edition
One discrete issue of the ProjectHub Newsletter. An edition can be drafted, scheduled, or sent; only sent editions are publicly discoverable.

## Newsletter digest item
A curated piece of content included in an edition, such as a newly launched Project, an upvoted Project, a community/forum highlight, or a ProjectHub announcement.

## Newsletter archive
The public collection of sent Newsletter editions, readable without a ProjectHub account.
